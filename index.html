What we believe

Salvation and deliverance are available to anyone through the death, burial and resurrection of Jesus. We receive it as Acts 2:38 describes.

We also teach by example that followers of Jesus can live soberly, righteously and godly in the world today.

  1. RepentTurn from sin and turn toward God.
  2. Be baptized in Jesus' nameWater baptism for the remission of sins.
  3. Be filled with the Holy SpiritEvidenced by speaking in other tongues as the Spirit gives utterance.
